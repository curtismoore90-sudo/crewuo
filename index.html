How to Connect

1

Download Classic Client or ClassicAssist/CUO

Ensure you have a clean installation of the Ultima Online classic client (Version 7.0.113 strongly recommended to ensure full content availability).

2

Configure Your Launcher

Open ClassicUO, Razor, or UOSteam and enter our server connection details:

Host: login.crewuo.com  |  Port: 2593
3

Create an Account

Account creation is automatic! Simply type in your desired username and password on the login screen to create your profile.
